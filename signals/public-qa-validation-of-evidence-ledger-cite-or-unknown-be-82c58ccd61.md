## What looked useful

Valid citation ids are not sufficient for public QA grounding: the literal string gate returned unsupported cited answers on 99/100 SQuAD-v2 plausible unanswerable cases, while a gold-support oracle reached 100% as an upper-bound sanity check.

## Boundaries and scale limits

CPU-only deterministic Tier 1 harness; candidate answers are SQuAD gold or plausible spans rather than generated by a real LLM/retriever; no semantic entailment verifier or human audit was tested.

## Claim scope

On 300 controlled SQuAD-v2 dev cases, a literal evidence-ledger gate that treats answer-string presence as citation support passes answerable and support-withheld checks but fails plausible-unanswerable cite-or-unknown behavior.

## Why it stopped

Tier 1 public QA direct test falsified the literal cite-or-unknown threshold: behavior success was 0.670 against a 0.950 target and unsupported-answer rate was 0.495 against a 0.050 maximum.

## Recommended next action

Stop this run as no-paper useful signal; next run should test real model-generated candidates plus a semantic support verifier on SQuAD-v2 or Natural Questions.
