## What looked useful

Reviewed doctrine cards improved from 10/12 to 12/12 correct versus raw trace nearest-neighbor memory with equal harmful rate, and shuffled doctrine actions failed to reproduce the effect, but the measured +16.7 point gain missed the +20 point threshold.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Small locally authored semi-real corpus; labels and doctrine were not independently blinded; no real incident commander workflow, public postmortem extraction pipeline, multi-reviewer agreement, or large-scale validation was tested.

## Claim scope

In a deterministic Tier 1 test on 12 held-out semi-real incident traces, reviewed doctrine memory selected safe first actions for all traces and avoided two raw trace nearest-neighbor cross-family confusions, but it did not meet the predeclared +20 point improvement threshold over raw trace memory.

## Why it stopped

No-paper closure: bounded direct Tier 1 evidence supports a mechanism but misses the predeclared effect-size threshold and lacks independent/blind trace provenance.

## Recommended next action

Run a blinded 50+ trace public-postmortem or approved-internal corpus test with frozen labels and independent doctrine review; stop paper work on the current small authored corpus.
