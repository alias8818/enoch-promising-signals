## Boundaries and scale limits

600 generated replay tasks, 5 seeds, 6 strategies, no live private production traces, no external human labels, no LLM-in-the-loop behavior variance, and no broad repository/task-family validation.

On a fixed-seed generated repeated-agent replay corpus with global, repo, task, stale, and cross-project doctrine conflicts, layered operator-doctrine memory recovered the correct current directive more accurately than no-memory, transcript-search, flat-retrieval, and layer-ablation controls.
