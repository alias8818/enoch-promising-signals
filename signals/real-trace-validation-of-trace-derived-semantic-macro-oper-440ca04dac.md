## What looked useful

A controlled real-trace test supports the mechanism that trace-derived semantic macro-operators capture reusable ordered structure beyond activity-frequency effects.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Single real process-mining corpus, one event-tokenization scheme, one macro learner, compression-only objective, no downstream model or planning task, no cross-domain robustness.

## Claim scope

On the BPIC 2012 real loan-application event log, BPE-style semantic macro-operators learned from training traces compress held-out traces by 85.2% net of dictionary cost and exceed a within-trace shuffled-order control by 2.48x.

## Why it stopped

Tier 1 direct validation succeeded and produced a useful mechanism signal, but evidence is not broad or downstream enough for paper readiness.

## Recommended next action

Run a medium confirmation on 3-5 heterogeneous real trace corpora with the same held-out compression protocol plus a downstream prediction metric before considering a bounded paper.
