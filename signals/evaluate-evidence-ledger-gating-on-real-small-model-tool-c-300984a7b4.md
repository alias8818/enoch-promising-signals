## What looked useful

A deterministic gate that requires cited current evidence rows to exactly support the model's answer produced a high-precision accepted subset from noisy real small-model tool-call-style outputs.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Single small model, one seed, synthetic compact ledgers, direct tool-result prompts rather than a full function-calling agent loop; measures conservative accepted-answer precision and coverage, not broad end-to-end tool-use reliability.

## Claim scope

On 48 controlled synthetic retrieve_evidence tool-result prompts generated by Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, a strict evidence-ledger citation gate accepted 31.25% of outputs with 100% accepted accuracy and zero stale false accepts, reducing accepted errors from 33 under no gate to 0.

## Why it stopped

Tier 1 direct test met the useful mechanism threshold, but evidence remains too narrow for publication readiness.

## Recommended next action

Run a bounded deepen follow-up across multiple small instruction models and seeds with an end-to-end retrieval tool loop plus adversarial citation ablations before considering any paper claim.
