## What looked useful

Tiered doctrine learned 24 macros per reusable-regime seed and avoided macro promotion in no-reuse controls. Primitive-cost macros shortened plans but did not reduce expansions or wall time. Unit-cost compressed macros reduced reusable-regime expansions by 98.50% versus primitive planning, but remained slightly worse than flat episodic replay on expansions and wall time.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Toy fact-set planner only; no neural model training, real operator logs, natural language doctrine, long-horizon tool use, or distribution-shift validation. Runs used at most 20 seeds, 80 training tasks per seed, and 40 held-out tasks per regime.

## Claim scope

Synthetic symbolic planning probe: support-threshold trace mining can promote recurring action subsequences into compressed macro-operators that reduce primitive planning expansions on held-out tasks with recurring motifs, but this did not beat a flat episodic replay baseline in the tested environment.

## Why it stopped

Bounded synthetic proxy supports a compressed-macro mechanism but does not support the broader claim that tiered reusable operator doctrine outperforms a simple flat episodic memory baseline.

## Recommended next action

Stop this run as no-paper useful signal; next bounded test should use compositional held-out tasks where nearest-neighbor episodic replay cannot solve by overlap and compare matched-budget episodic, doctrine, and hybrid retrieval.
