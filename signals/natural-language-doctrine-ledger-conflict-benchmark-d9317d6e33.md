## What looked useful

## Boundaries and scale limits

The benchmark is synthetic and template-bounded: three doctrine phrasings, fixed vocabulary, explicit numeric thresholds, one exception clause, and no real legal/compliance corpus, ambiguous wording, nested precedence, or human annotation variance.

## Claim scope

In a controlled synthetic benchmark of 96 natural-language-like doctrine statements and 3072 ledger events, a template compiler that extracts actor/action/resource/context/threshold/exception fields detects doctrine-event conflicts more accurately than keyword and threshold-blind text controls.
