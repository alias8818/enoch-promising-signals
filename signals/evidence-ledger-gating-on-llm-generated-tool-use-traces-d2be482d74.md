## What looked useful

Evidence-ledger gating is useful but incomplete: ledger-only verification blocked 85.7% of unsupported cases and failed the 90% threshold due to text/ledger mismatch, while output-bound ledger verification blocked 100% with 0% false blocks on supported cases.

## Boundaries and scale limits

The corpus is deterministic and synthetic; final answers were not generated by live LLM inference, claim normalization was assumed, and output binding used exact text containment rather than robust span or semantic alignment.

## Claim scope

On a seeded controlled corpus of 240 synthetic tool-use traces, a gate that checks evidence IDs and normalized subject/predicate/value claims improves substantially over no-gate and citation-only baselines, but only an output-bound evidence-ledger gate meets the predeclared Tier 1 threshold.

## Why it stopped

Tier 1 controlled direct test produced a useful mechanism signal but not publication-grade evidence; the ledger-only gate missed a realistic text/ledger mismatch failure mode.

## Recommended next action

Run a bounded deepen test on actual LLM-generated tool-use traces where the model emits both visible final answers and machine-checkable ledger records, with independent adjudication of visible-claim support.
