## What looked useful

The strongest encoder, sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 used through Hugging Face AutoModel, achieved lexical full-text top-1 0.667, embedding full-text 0.750, embedding summary 0.694, and hybrid lexical plus full/summary embeddings 0.806. The hybrid had 5 condition-only correct answers and 0 lexical-only losses versus lexical full-text, but exact paired p=0.0625 on only 36 queries. A weaker bert-tiny encoder showed hybrid improvement but embedding-only and summary-only underperformed, indicating encoder dependence.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Synthetic doctrine corpus; only 12 entries and 36 queries; compressed summaries were authored in the harness rather than generated by a live LLM; no live agent memory loop, real doctrine corpus, large retrieval store, adversarial paraphrases, or publication-scale robustness testing.

## Claim scope

In a controlled synthetic doctrine-memory retrieval task with 12 doctrine entries and 36 paraphrased labeled queries, embedding and summary-aware hybrid retrieval improved top-1 accuracy over lexical full-text retrieval for two small encoder runs, with the strongest run improving from 24/36 to 29/36 correct.

## Why it stopped

Tier 1 controlled evidence produced a useful mechanism signal, but the result is small, synthetic, and mixed rather than publication-grade.

## Recommended next action

Run a bounded medium direct test on a real doctrine corpus with LLM-generated summaries, at least 100 labeled paraphrase queries, multiple encoders, and summary-length/distractor ablations before considering any paper claim.
