## What looked useful

Across 50,000 synthetic tasks, baseline execution produced 18.30% unsafe executions among executed proposals, while the ledger gate allowed 0 unsafe executions and detected 100% of tamper probes. The main cost was availability: false rejects were 40.36% at 18% evidence omission, and the ablation showed false rejects rising from 7.19% at 0% omission to 57.75% at 30% omission.

## Boundaries and scale limits

No real 70M-parameter model was run; no real home devices, sensors, user consent UX, natural-language parsing, or deployment latency were tested. The evidence supports only the ledger/gating mechanism under synthetic proposal and evidence distributions.

## Claim scope

In a deterministic synthetic home-agent benchmark, an append-only evidence ledger with fresh sensor evidence, capability evidence, risky-action consent, and hash-chain verification prevented all unsafe or unsupported executions generated by a seeded proxy proposal policy.

## Why it stopped

Useful synthetic mechanism signal only; this is not a full validation of 70M-parameter home agents and should not be treated as paper-ready.

## Recommended next action

Run a bounded deepen test with a real 70M-class local model emitting structured action proposals on this same benchmark, and stop unless unsafe allows remain 0 with false rejects below 25% at realistic evidence-capture rates.
