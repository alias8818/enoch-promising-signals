## What looked useful

Evidence-aware anchor auditing beat retrieval overlap and short-context ablations but lost to a claim-only Naive Bayes baseline, indicating mutation-artifact leakage; path/status checks have useful signal, number checks are weak.

## Boundaries and scale limits

Unsupported examples are synthetic natural mutations, not independently observed real agent unsupported claims or human-authored adversarial claims; evaluation is local CPU-only over Enoch/Codex logs and does not establish deployed-agent generality.

## Claim scope

Medium local evaluation of deterministic unsupported-claim auditing on 6,307 natural-language claim/evidence examples extracted from 424 real Codex/Enoch tool-call ledgers, with unsupported labels generated by anchor mutations of supported claims.

## Why it stopped

Tier 2 fixed-seed evaluation failed the real-baseline bar: claim-only Naive Bayes outperformed the evidence-aware anchor audit on held-out projects (balanced accuracy 0.703 vs 0.679; AUROC 0.795 vs 0.669).

## Recommended next action

Stop this run as no-paper useful signal; next run should collect independently labeled natural unsupported claims and require evidence-aware auditing to beat claim-only artifact controls.
